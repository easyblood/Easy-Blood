What is Cholesterol?

Cholesterol is a waxy substance found in your blood. While your body needs some cholesterol to build healthy cells, having high levels can increase your risk of heart disease.

Symptoms of High Cholesterol

  • Generally no symptoms until it's too late
  • Chest pain or discomfort (when heart disease develops)
  • Fatty deposits around eyes or joints (in severe cases)

Causes of High Cholesterol

  • Poor diet (high in saturated fats and trans fats)
  • Obesity
  • Lack of physical activity
  • Genetics
  • Age (cholesterol levels rise as you age)

Diagnosis of High Cholesterol

A blood test known as a lipid panel measures total cholesterol, LDL (bad cholesterol), HDL (good cholesterol), and triglycerides.

Management and Treatment

  • Dietary changes (lowering saturated and trans fats)
  • Exercise (increasing physical activity)
  • Medications (statins or other cholesterol-lowering drugs)

Home Remedies for High Cholesterol

  • Increase soluble fiber intake (e.g., oats, beans, lentils)
  • Eat healthy fats (e.g., olive oil, avocado, nuts)
  • Exercise regularly (at least 30 minutes per day)
  • Quit smoking and reduce alcohol consumption
This information is for general understanding. Always consult a healthcare professional for diagnosis and treatment.
