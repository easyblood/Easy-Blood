What is PCOD?

Polycystic Ovarian Disease (PCOD) is a hormonal disorder that affects women, where ovaries develop numerous small cysts and disrupt the normal ovulation process.

Symptoms of PCOD

  • Irregular periods
  • Excessive hair growth (hirsutism)
  • Acne and oily skin
  • Weight gain or difficulty losing weight
  • Thinning hair or hair loss

Causes of PCOD

  • Hormonal imbalance
  • Genetics (family history)
  • Insulin resistance
  • Increased levels of androgens (male hormones)

Diagnosis of PCOD

Diagnosis typically involves blood tests, ultrasound, and a review of symptoms to identify irregularities in hormone levels, ovaries, and menstrual cycle.

Management and Treatment

  • Medications to regulate periods (birth control pills or progestins)
  • Insulin-sensitizing drugs (like Metformin)
  • Anti-androgen medications to reduce excessive hair growth
  • Lifestyle changes (diet and exercise)

Home Remedies for PCOD

  • Maintain a healthy diet, rich in fiber and low in refined sugars
  • Exercise regularly to improve insulin sensitivity
  • Try herbal remedies like cinnamon and spearmint tea
  • Manage stress through yoga and meditation
This information is for general understanding. Always consult a healthcare professional for diagnosis and treatment.
