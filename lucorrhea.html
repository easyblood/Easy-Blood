What is Leucorrhea?

Leucorrhea refers to the white or yellowish vaginal discharge that is typically odorless. It is often caused by infections, hormonal imbalances, or other medical conditions.

Symptoms of Leucorrhea

  • White or yellowish discharge from the vagina
  • Itching or irritation in the vaginal area
  • Unpleasant odor from the discharge
  • Pelvic pain or discomfort

Causes of Leucorrhea

  • Infections (bacterial or fungal)
  • Hormonal imbalances
  • Poor hygiene
  • Sexually transmitted infections (STIs)
  • Pregnancy or menstruation-related changes

Diagnosis of Leucorrhea

A doctor may perform pelvic exams, laboratory tests, and examine discharge samples to determine the cause of leucorrhea.

Management and Treatment

  • Antibiotic or antifungal medications (if caused by infections)
  • Hormonal therapy (in case of hormonal imbalances)
  • Maintaining proper hygiene
  • Wearing breathable cotton undergarments

Home Remedies for Leucorrhea

  • Drinking plenty of water to stay hydrated
