What are Pimples?

Pimples are small, red, and often painful bumps that appear on the skin, often caused by clogged hair follicles and oil buildup. They are common during puberty but can occur at any age.

Symptoms of Pimples

  • Red, swollen bumps on the skin
  • White or blackheads
  • Painful or tender areas on the skin
  • Occasional pus-filled bumps

Causes of Pimples

  • Excess oil production
  • Clogged hair follicles
  • Bacterial infection
  • Hormonal changes (e.g., during puberty)
  • Stress and diet

Diagnosis of Pimples

Pimples can usually be diagnosed by visual examination. In severe cases, a dermatologist may perform additional tests to determine underlying causes or other skin conditions.

Management and Treatment

  • Topical creams with benzoyl peroxide or salicylic acid
  • Prescription medications (in severe cases)
  • Regular cleansing of the skin to avoid clogged pores
  • Use of oil-free skincare products

Home Remedies for Pimples

  • Applying aloe vera gel to reduce inflammation
  • Using honey as a natural antibacterial agent
  • Applying ice packs to reduce swelling
  • Using tea tree oil as an antimicrobial solution
This information is for general understanding. Always consult a healthcare professional for diagnosis and treatment.
