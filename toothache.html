What is Toothache?

A toothache is pain around the teeth and jaws that can be caused by a variety of dental problems such as tooth decay, gum disease, or an infection.

Symptoms of Toothache

  • Sharp or throbbing pain in the tooth
  • Pain when chewing or touching the tooth
  • Swollen gums around the affected tooth
  • Bad breath or unpleasant taste in the mouth
  • Fever or headache (in some cases)

Causes of Toothache

  • Tooth decay or cavities
  • Gum disease (gingivitis or periodontitis)
  • Tooth abscess or infection
  • Cracked or broken tooth
  • Exposed tooth roots

Diagnosis of Toothache

A dentist typically diagnoses the cause of toothache by performing a physical examination, checking X-rays, and evaluating the patient’s symptoms.

Management and Treatment

  • Over-the-counter pain relievers like ibuprofen or acetaminophen
  • Antibiotics (if there’s an infection)
  • Dental procedures like fillings, root canals, or extractions
  • Home remedies like salt water rinses or cold compress

Home Remedies for Toothache

  • Salt water rinse to reduce inflammation
  • Applying a cold compress to the cheek near the affected tooth
  • Clove oil or garlic paste to numb the pain
  • Drinking warm tea with chamomile to soothe the pain
This information is for general understanding. Always consult a healthcare professional for diagnosis and treatment.
