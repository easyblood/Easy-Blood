PCOD - Polycystic Ovary Disorder

PCOD is a common condition affecting women's ovaries. Symptoms include irregular periods, acne, and weight gain. Early diagnosis and treatment are key to managing the condition.

Symptoms

  • Irregular menstrual cycles
  • Excess hair growth (hirsutism)
  • Infertility
  • Acne and oily skin

Treatment

PCOD is managed through medication, lifestyle changes, and weight management.
