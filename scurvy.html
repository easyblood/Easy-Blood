What is Scurvy?

Scurvy is a disease caused by a deficiency of vitamin C, which is necessary for the synthesis of collagen in the body. It results in symptoms like fatigue, swollen gums, and easy bruising.

Symptoms of Scurvy

  • Fatigue
  • Swollen, bleeding gums
  • Joint pain
  • Easy bruising
  • Rough, dry skin
  • Hair loss

Causes of Scurvy

  • Vitamin C deficiency due to poor diet
  • Inadequate intake of fruits and vegetables
  • Malabsorption disorders
  • Chronic alcohol use

Diagnosis of Scurvy

Scurvy is diagnosed through clinical symptoms, blood tests to check vitamin C levels, and other assessments by a healthcare professional.

Management and Treatment

  • Vitamin C supplementation
  • Dietary changes to include more fruits and vegetables
  • Oral or intravenous vitamin C if needed

Home Remedies for Scurvy

  • Increase intake of citrus fruits like oranges, lemons, and grapefruits
  • Consume vegetables like bell peppers, tomatoes, and spinach
  • Include berries and kiwi in the diet
  • Drink fresh fruit juices like orange juice
This information is for general understanding. Always consult a healthcare professional for diagnosis and treatment.
